Our story

About Us

A quieter way to wear something meaningful.

CrystalMuse began as a student project at City University of Hong Kong with one question: what if choosing a crystal felt less like a transaction, and more like choosing something that already belongs to you?

Rose quartz · the stone of gentle things

Our story

Built around three ideas

Most crystal shops ask you to choose from what is already on the shelf. We wanted the shelf to be a starting point instead. So the site does three things at once: it sells small, honest pieces; it lets you design a bracelet bead by bead; and it gives you somewhere to read, browse and play without being sold to.

We are not a wellness brand. We do not promise calmer sleep, better luck or a different life. What we can offer is a well-made object, a clear description of it, and the stories people have told about these stones for a very long time — offered as culture, not as cure.

This website is a course prototype for a commercial site concept. Product data, readings and checkout are simulated to demonstrate structure and user flow.

What we stand for

Three promises we can keep

01

Personal by design

Colour, size, stone, sequence. Nothing is locked — the piece is finished when you say it is.

02

Honest by default

Material and treatment are stated plainly. Where a meaning is cultural rather than scientific, we say so on the page.

03

Gentle by nature

Soft palettes, small batches, no pressure to buy. You are welcome to look and leave.

How we work

Editorial principles

01

Describe the object first

Stone type, bead size, finish and what is included — before any story.

02

Label symbolism as symbolism

“Believed to bring calm” is a cultural note, not a health claim. We phrase it that way.

03

Readings stay entertainment

Tarot and zodiac content is for reflection. It is not medical, legal, financial or psychological advice.

04

Custom is confirmed before making

You approve the summary and the price before anything is cut, strung or packed.
